# Relationship Method

**Executive coverage score** (account level, 0–100) = 100 × Σ over key customer executives of (weight × strength ÷ 3) ÷ Σ weights.

Weights: economic buyer 3 · executive sponsor 3 · business-unit heads in scope 2 · other C-level 1.

| Score | Label |
|---|---|
| ≥ 70 | Strong |
| 40–69 | Adequate |
| < 40 | Insufficient |

**Sponsor dependency**: exactly one key executive at strength ≥ 2 on a strategic account.

**Deteriorating**: interactions in the last 90 days vs the prior 90 give a robust z ≤ −2, or buyer-initiated share falls ≥ 20 percentage points.

**Departure verification** (at least one required):
- email bounce plus a signature or auto-reply stating the move;
- a statement by the customer;
- a cited public source dated within 30 days.

Never infer a departure from silence alone.
