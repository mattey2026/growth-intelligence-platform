# Business Model Taxonomy and Skill Applicability (v6.0)

| Code | Model | Key evidence |
|---|---|---|
| B2B | Business to business | Organizational buyers with roles (CIO, CFO, procurement); high revenue per customer; contracts; pipeline stages |
| B2G | Business to government | Agencies, tenders, bids, awards, public budgets |
| B2C | Business to consumer | Many individual customers; orders, baskets, low revenue per customer |
| D2C | Direct to consumer (a B2C subtype) | Own brand sold through own website, app, or social channels; ROAS; checkout |
| B2B2C | Via business partners to consumers | Resellers, distributors, sell-through, end consumers |
| C2C | Consumer to consumer (marketplace) | Buyer and seller are both individuals; listings; GMV; take rate |
| B2E | Business to employee | Employees as customers (benefits, internal portals) |
| C2B | Consumer to business | Individuals supplying businesses (creators, freelancers) |
| G2C / G2G | Government to citizen / government | Citizens, permits, claims / inter-agency transfers |
| Hybrid | Several models | Report the primary model plus the secondary ones |

## Skill applicability
| Skill group | B2B | B2G | D2C/B2C | B2B2C | Marketplace |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platform (context, memory, data, knowledge, twin*, orchestrator, BI copilot, watch, command center, market, briefing) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Deal, pipeline and forecast, relationship, RFP, meetings, pricing (deal mode), account strategist | ✓ | ✓ (bids) | — | Partner deals | — |
| Renewal and retention, opportunity discovery, competitive, win/loss | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (cohort retention; category competitors) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Pricing (portfolio mode), scenario planner | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

*The twin represents a *customer*: an account in B2B, or a segment or cohort in B2C, where individual-consumer twins are not built by default for privacy reasons.

**Coverage gap (roadmap):** consumer-specific engines (cohort retention, marketing-mix / ROAS, basket analysis) are v6 roadmap items. Today, D2C and B2C are served by the platform skills, the adaptive KPIs, the BI copilot, and the scenario planner.
