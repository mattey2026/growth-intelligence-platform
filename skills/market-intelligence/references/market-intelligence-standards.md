# Market Intelligence Standards

## Query matrix (driven by the Business Context Profile)
| Business model | Market questions | Benchmark KPIs |
|---|---|---|
| B2B (enterprise tech / services) | Sector spend growth by region; pricing and discount trends; competitor launches, deals, layoffs, M&A; regulation affecting customers' industries | Gross margin, NRR/GRR, win rate, sales cycle, CAC payback (public-company disclosures, industry surveys) |
| B2G | Public budgets and appropriations; procurement rule changes; award trends; incumbents | Award rate, recompete win rate, margin on government contracts |
| D2C / B2C | Category demand; consumer sentiment and spending; ad-cost (CPM/CPC) trends; channel shifts; returns norms | Conversion, AOV, CAC, ROAS, repeat rate, return rate |
| B2B2C | Channel partner health; sell-through; retail demand | Sell-through, partner concentration |
| Marketplace (C2C) | Supply/demand liquidity; take-rate trends; regulation | GMV growth, take rate, repeat buyers |

## Source quality order
1. Regulators, statistics offices, central banks.
2. Company filings and releases.
3. Industry bodies.
4. Reputable research firms (public summaries).
5. Established business press.

Never use forums, SEO listicles, or undated pages.

## Freshness windows
| Content | Window |
|---|---|
| Competitor moves, pricing, demand | 90 days |
| Market growth, regulation | 12 months |
| Structural benchmarks | 24 months |

Anything older is marked **stale** in memory and re-researched when it is needed.

## Like-for-like rule
Compare only matching definitions: the same metric, scope, period, and segment. If there is no like-for-like benchmark, show the finding as context only, without a comparison.
