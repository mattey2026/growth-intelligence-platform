# Output Templates

Every output ends with: **Evidence coverage** (share of material recommendations with cited evidence) · **Sources and coverage** · **Data gaps** · **Audit block** (if actions were proposed) · **Ledger entries** (if predictions were made).

## §1 Executive Account Brief (1 page)
```
<ACCOUNT> — Executive Brief · <date> · Internal – Confidential
CURRENT STATE: revenue <$, trend> · products <n> · contract <end date> · health <label: drivers> · pipeline <$>
KEY DEVELOPMENTS (since <date>): 3 bullets, each with its source
TOP RISKS: 3 (dimension, level, evidence, $ at risk)
TOP OPPORTUNITIES: 3 (size, probability, basis)
PREDICTIONS: renewal <p, band, confidence> · expansion <p> · revenue at risk <$>
RECOMMENDED ACTIONS: 3–5 (owner, due, approval needed?)
DECISIONS REQUIRED FROM LEADERSHIP
```

## §2 Strategic Account Plan
1. Account objectives
2. Current state
3. SWOT (evidence tables)
4. Stakeholder map and relationship strategy
5. Desired future state and revenue ambition bridge
6. Growth strategy and whitespace table
7. Opportunity map and opportunity strategies
8. Competitive landscape
9. Risk dashboard and risk plan
10. 30/60/90-day action plan
11. Predictions
12. Measures of success
13. Data gaps

Offer it as a document, a deck (Slides), or a workbook.

## §3 Account Review (QBR-style, internal)
What changed (from change intelligence) · Performance vs plan (variance bridge) · Pipeline and forecast (with slip risk) · Risks (up or down) · Opportunities (new or progressed) · Action-plan status (done, late, blocked) · Accuracy of previous predictions · Decisions required.

## §4 Account Deep Dive
All sections, with full evidence tables, source-by-source findings, cross-system conflicts, methodology notes, and the appendix of metrics with formulas.

## §5 Account Change Intelligence
```
<ACCOUNT> — Changes <prev date> → <current date>
STRATEGY REVIEW NEEDED: Yes/No — why
MATERIAL CHANGES (ranked by impact): change · why it matters · SWOT/risk/prediction affected · action
SWOT CHANGES · RISK CHANGES · PREDICTION CHANGES · STAKEHOLDER CHANGES · NEW OPPORTUNITIES
RECOMMENDED ADJUSTMENTS TO THE PLAN
```

## §6 Portfolio Prioritization
A table of account · tier · score · top drivers · weakest criteria · rank range under weight changes · top-quartile stability · recommended coverage model. Follow it with the weights used, and how the ranking changes under alternative weights.
