# Account Risk Framework: 9 Dimensions

Level each dimension **Low / Medium / High / Critical** from the signals present. The thresholds are defaults; replace them with company values. Always show the signals, evidence, why the risk matters, and the trend vs the last snapshot.

| Dimension | Early-warning signals (default thresholds) | High when | Critical when | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Revenue down >10% YoY; downsell requests; usage down >15% over 90 days | 2 signals | Renewal ≤ 180 days + 2 signals | Protects the base |
| Relationship | Economic buyer not engaged in 120 days; buyer-initiated activity down >50% | 1 signal on a strategic account | Executive sponsor departed with no replacement | Relationships precede revenue |
| Competitive | Competitor mentioned in 2+ interactions; competitor pilot; RFI/RFP issued | Pilot or RFP | RFP on our incumbent scope | Displacement |
| Service | Sev-1/2 open >14 days; case volume up >50% QoQ; SLA breaches | 1 signal | Executive escalation | Service issues precede churn |
| Contract | Expiry ≤ 180 days without renewal opportunity; termination-for-convenience clause; notice window open | 1 signal | Notice window ≤ 60 days and at risk | Hard dates |
| Financial | DSO >60, disputes, credit hold; customer financial distress | 1 signal | Credit hold or insolvency signal | Cash and collectability |
| Stakeholder | Champion or sponsor departure; reorganization; single-threaded | 1 signal | Champion and sponsor both lost | Loss of access |
| Delivery | Milestones late; project RAG red; resource gaps | Red project | Contract penalty exposure | Reputation, margin |
| Strategic | Customer strategy moving away from our category; M&A; vendor consolidation stated | 1 signal | Stated exit from our category | Long-term fit |

**Customer health** is the explained composite of these dimensions. Report it as a label plus its drivers, never as a bare number.
