# 11. Detailed Specifications: First 5 Skills

These specifications follow the 24-item design standard. Each skill's `SKILL.md` is the executable version; `references/skill-design-card.md` in each skill carries the same content.

| Item | data-intelligence | deal-intelligence | pipeline-forecast-intelligence | account-intelligence-swot-planning | daily-growth-briefing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Business problem** | Data is fragmented, unmapped, duplicated, stale, and conflicting, so every analysis starts with manual wrangling | Deal reviews interrogate sellers rather than evidence; close plans are generic; slippage is found late | Pipeline reports describe but don't predict; forecast risk and what-if are handled in spreadsheets | Account plans are static, generic, and slow; risks and whitespace are missed | Sellers and leaders must hunt across systems for what changed; signals are missed |
| **Persona** | Everyone; RevOps and analysts as power users | AEs, managers, deal teams | Managers, RevOps, CRO, Finance | Strategic AMs, AEs, CSMs, executives | AEs, managers, CSMs, executives, RevOps |
| **Trigger** | File uploaded; "analyze this"; a new source connected; a data-quality question | "Review/help me win/close plan for <deal>"; red deal flagged | "Will we hit the number"; forecast call; "what if deal X slips" | "Account plan / SWOT / QBR / what changed / prioritize accounts" | Morning schedule; "what should I focus on today" |
| **Inputs** | Files, connectors, question | Opportunity; history; evidence | Scope, period, target, opportunities, history | Account(s), mode, period, prior snapshot | User identity and role, book of accounts and deals |
| **Data sources** | Any source | CRM, email/calendar, transcripts, documents, CPQ | CRM or forecast tool, warehouse, finance | CRM, ERP, ITSM, customer success, CLM, email, files, web | Outputs of every monitor, plus email and calendar |
| **Connectors / tools** | Profiler, normalize, entity_resolver, ts_forecast, anomaly | deal_risk_model, buying-committee, deal-strategy, deal-desk | pipeline_whatif, forecast_sim, deal_risk_model, ts_forecast, anomaly | whitespace, prioritizer, change_diff, propensity, ts_forecast | signal_ranker; all monitors |
| **Context requirements** | Fiscal calendar, currency, stage ladder, definitions | Sales methodology, stage exit criteria, approval matrix | Quota/target, fiscal periods, stage rates, correlation assumption | Offering catalogue, segments, ambition, weights | Role, owned entities, preferences, quiet hours |
| **Analytics** | Profiling, KPIs, trends, segmentation, variance, correlation | Velocity, stage progression, time in stage vs peers, evidence coverage | Coverage, quality, aging, velocity, conversion, leakage, concentration, rep exposure | Trend, variance, cohort vs peers, correlation, anomaly | Change detection across monitors |
| **Predictive** | Period forecasts; anomalies | Win and slip probability; close-date risk; cycle-time quantiles | P10/P50/P90; probability of hitting target; forecast error; deal-level scenarios | Churn, renewal, expansion, cross-sell/upsell, revenue at risk, displacement, attrition | Inherits predictions from the source skills |
| **Reasoning** | Infer field meaning; resolve identities; reconcile conflicts | Model vs evidence reconciliation; decision-process mapping | Which deals drive the variance; which assumptions matter | Signals → patterns → risks → opportunities | Why each signal matters to this user |
| **Decision logic** | System-of-record rules; confidence thresholds; blocking questions | Red-flag rules R1–R9; walk-away test; approval path | Go-live bar; scenario deltas; swing-deal ranking | SWOT anti-generic test; 9-dimension risk rules; prioritization | value × urgency × confidence × novelty × relevance |
| **Recommendations** | Fixes, clean-up, next analyses | Deal strategy, close plan, execution plan | Inspect swing deals; pipeline actions; resource moves | Growth, relationship, and risk strategies; 30/60/90 actions | Top 3–7 actions, each with "Claude can do this" |
| **Actions** | Clean files; approved write-back of hygiene fixes | Tasks, close-date and category proposals, mutual-plan draft, meeting request draft | None (never submits a forecast); tasks on request | Tasks, opportunities, drafts, plan documents | Prepares the underlying actions; one-click hand-off to the owning skill |
| **Approval** | Every write-back | Every field change and communication | Any published number | Every write and communication | Each action, in the owning skill |
| **Output** | Data intelligence report + canonical files | Deal Review → Strategy → Close Plan → Execution Plan | Pipeline health, forecast range, what-if table, call agenda | Brief, plan, review, deep dive, change report, portfolio | Daily Growth Briefing |
| **Evidence** | Lineage per row | Cited interactions and field history | Deal-level inputs and sources | ≥ 90% evidence coverage reported | Evidence and source skill per item |
| **Confidence** | Mapping confidence; data-quality score | Backtest-based (standards §4) | Model and sample based; scenarios labelled illustrative | Per SWOT item and prediction | Carried from the source |
| **Error handling** | Ambiguous dates, subtotals, merged headers → ask or exclude | Thin history → rules; conflicts shown | Thin history → wider ranges; missing probabilities → stage rates, disclosed | Sparse data → gaps first | A failed monitor is listed, never hidden |
| **Security** | Masking; per-source permissions | Hierarchy scoping | Confidential (MNPI) marking | Internal-only stance and risk content | Only the user's entitled data |
| **Auditability** | Mapping report + audit block | Change sets + ledger | Ledger of ranges | Snapshot + ledger | Log of delivered signals and actions taken |
| **Baseline** | Manual wrangling time; issues found manually | Seller-commit slip AUC; review prep time | Historical forecast error by week of quarter | Research and plan-preparation time; plan outcomes | Time to find "what changed"; signals missed |
| **Success metrics** | Time to insight −70%; issues caught; mapping accuracy | Slipped value flagged ≥ 4 weeks early; win rate; prep time | Error at weeks 4/8/12 vs baseline; P10–P90 hit rate | −70% research; −50% prep; ≥ 90% evidence coverage | Actions taken; lead time on risks; minutes saved |
| **Test scenarios** | Messy workbook; ambiguous dates; CRM↔ERP conflicts | Commit deal with no paper process; competitive stuck deal | Deal A slips / B lost / rep unavailable; new product | Full plan; generic-SWOT refusal; sponsor loss | AE morning; exec morning; no signals; failed source |
| **Failure modes** | Wrong mapping accepted; silent merges | Over-trust in the model; stale evidence | False precision; correlated misses | Generic content; invented potential | Noise overload; stale signals; alert fatigue |
