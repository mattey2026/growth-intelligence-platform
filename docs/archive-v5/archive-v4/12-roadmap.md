# 12. MVP-to-Enterprise Roadmap

| Phase | Timeline | Scope | Exit criteria (measured vs baseline) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0: Prove on files** | Weeks 0–6 | data-intelligence + deal-intelligence + pipeline-forecast on **exported** history from any CRM; baseline studies | Deal model AUC ≥ 0.70 and better than seller commit; time-to-insight −50%; mapping accuracy ≥ 95% on audited fields |
| **P1: Connected MVP** | Months 2–4 | Live CRM, email/calendar, and transcript connectors; account strategist; daily briefing; meeting skills; win-loss, lead triage, revenue leakage, territory/quota/capacity, customer economics | Pilot vs control: prep −50%, slipped value flagged ≥ 4 weeks early, briefing actions per user per week, adoption ≥ 60% WAU |
| **P2: Cross-functional** | Months 4–9 | ERP, ITSM, and customer-success connectors; growth-signal-orchestrator; executive-growth-command-center; QBR builder; service and case intelligence; marketing attribution; competitive intelligence | Service → sales interventions measured; NRR/GRR vs control; forecast error reduction |
| **P3: Enterprise OS** | Months 9–18 | Canonical warehouse layer; ABAC; model registry; ledger-driven recalibration; Operations, IT, ITSM, and Strategy skills; workflow-automation actions | Monthly model governance report; audited ROI; multi-business-unit rollout |

**Principle.** Breadth follows proof. A skill enters a phase only after the one before it has shown a measured improvement.
