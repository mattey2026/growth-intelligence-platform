# 6. Market Intelligence

Queries come from the Business Context Profile (model × industry × geography × scale), not generic news. Sources follow the quality order and are always cited and dated. Freshness windows: 90 days for competitors and pricing, 12 months for markets and regulation, 24 months for structural benchmarks. Comparisons are like-for-like only. Findings are stored in memory as observations with a next-review date. See `skills/market-intelligence/references/market-intelligence-standards.md`.

The test workbook run did not include live market research. External benchmarks will fill the "Benchmark" field on the command-center cards once they are researched.
